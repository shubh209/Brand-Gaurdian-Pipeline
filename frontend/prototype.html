Violations (4) SlimFast Pro — Burn Fat 3X Faster
Critical

00:02

"I lost 30 pounds in just 2 weeks"

Unsubstantiated weight loss claim. FTC requires competent and reliable scientific evidence for weight loss claims. 30 lbs in 14 days is physiologically implausible without medical intervention.

Policy Citation

FTC Dietary Supplements Guide: "Advertisers must have adequate substantiation for all claims before disseminating..."

Suggested Rewrite

"I achieved noticeable results with SlimFast Pro as part of a balanced diet and exercise program. Individual results may vary."

Critical

00:05

"It literally melts fat while you sleep"

Unsubstantiated mechanism claim. No dietary supplement can "melt fat" during sleep without any behavioral change. This implies a drug-like mechanism without FDA approval.

Suggested Rewrite

"SlimFast Pro supports your weight management goals when combined with healthy lifestyle choices."

Critical

00:08

"No exercise needed, no diet changes"

Misleading efficacy claim. Claims that weight loss requires no effort violate YouTube's misleading claims policy and FTC substantiation requirements.

Warning

00:15

"Use code SLIM50 for 50% off today only"

Time-limited offer without disclosure of typical availability. If this offer is recurring, the "today only" language may constitute deceptive urgency.
